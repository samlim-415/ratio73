A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Is ratio73 a skincare brand or a retailer?

We’re a curated retailer of Korean skincare brands. We select and source products from established brands, starting with Centellian24. ratio73 is our approach to choosing a range, not the manufacturer of the products.

Where will you ship?

Our initial launch markets are Australia and the United States, with more countries planned over time. Exact shipping coverage and delivery options will be confirmed before orders open.

Where are the trending products?

We’re starting small with one core product. The 70/30 balance is our plan for the growing range. Our first trending picks will follow as we expand.

Can I order yet?

Not quite. This is a preview of the ratio73 experience. You can explore the product and try the bag, but prices are illustrative and orders, payments and email signups are not live.
