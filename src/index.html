FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things to know

What is ratio73?

ratio73 is a Korean skincare retailer concept. We plan to sell products from established brands, beginning with Centellian24. We don’t manufacture the products.

Where will ratio73 deliver?

We plan to launch in Australia and the United States. We’ll confirm delivery areas and shipping costs before orders open.

Why only one product?

We’re starting with Centellian24 Madeca Cream Time Reverse. As the range expands, we’re aiming for 70% established favourites and 30% new or trending skincare products.

Can I place an order?

Not yet. This is a preview site. Prices are examples, and the bag is for demonstration only. Orders, payments and email signup are not enabled.
